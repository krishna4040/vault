# Trend Identification

- uptrend
- downtrend
- sideways or consolidation trend

Rules:

- Trend is the friend
- never trade against the trend
- there are various trading strategies like reversal, but they are difficult
- buy in uptrend for investment and book profit on downtrend
- we can view the different trends using `supertrend filter`

`How to know strength of trend change`
We use `adx filter` if adx > 25 then the trend change is significant
