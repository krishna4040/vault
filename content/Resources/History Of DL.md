# Reticular Theory

Joseph von gerlach peoposed that the nervous system is a single continuous network as opposed to a network of many discrete cells!

# Staining Technique

Camillo Gogli discovered a chemical reaction that allowed him to examine nervous tissue in much greater detail than ever before
He was a proponent of Reticular theory.

# Neuron Doctrine

Santiago Ramon y Cajal used Golgi's technique to study nervous system and proposed that it is actually made up of discrete individual cells forming a network

# The Term Neuron

The term neuron was coined by Heinrivh Wilhelm Gottfried von Waldeyer-Hartz around 1891
He further consilidated the Neuron Doctrin
