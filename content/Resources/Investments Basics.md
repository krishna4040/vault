# Stocks vs Mutual funds vs ETFs

## Stocks

- buying companies shares
- control in our hand
- no expert, requires time, learn
- calculated risk, more profit

## Mutual funds

- our money goes to fund manager appointed by company whos mf we buy
- no control, company needs to put money irrespective of market situation
- expert advice at cost of expense ratio and exit load
- safe play

# ETF SIP Investment

Here we have control and we can time the entry and exit, so a slight
modification in SIP technique can give a enourmous returns
we invest when index is -ve, and when it is +ve we do not invest but
when it is next -ve we invest the whole amount of +ve days as well
that day
A slight modification can be looking at the % of index
This is known as `buying on dip`
we can get 1cr in 10yrs by just getting 20%-50% using pleding and intraday

# FNO

## Optioins

In FNO we can become:

- option seller - sells insurance to option buyers
- option buyer - buys insurance from option sellers

The insurance here are:

- CE - call european
- PE - put european

## Purpose 1: Hedging

We buy shares in speculation that the market goes up, and buy a corresponding put option to insure our investment, if in case the market goes down.
