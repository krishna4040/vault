# Basics

price is the amount we pay for an asset and value is the amount we get from the asset.
value investing is when we buy any stock at its intrinstic price,
but not always an asset is available at its intristic price so we come to `ratio analysis`

# Ratios

- PE Ratio - price to earning
- PB Ratio - price to booking
- Debt Ratio - Debt to Equity
- Current Ratio

## PE/PB

if PE of a company is x, then we are paying x amount to earn rupee 1 to the company or asset
PE less than 20 is good
The less the PE the better and same for PB

## Debt/Equity

Debt is cheaper than equity for an estabilished company
for startups equity is cheaper, if repaying capacity debt is good
it should be less, but if repaying capacity is good then do not matter

## Current ratio

It is defiend as current assets / current libalities
The more the current ratio the better
