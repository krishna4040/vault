> [!info]
> Zettelkasten is a german word for **slip box**

## Key principles

- create atomic or short form notes
- use unique identifiers to label notes
- link notes
- write in your own words
- cite for sources
- keep non hierarchical notes non categorial
- Review notes
- Publish your notes turn into content

## Advantages

- increase creative thinking
- improve understanding of ideas
- makes content creation easier
- long term knowledge building
