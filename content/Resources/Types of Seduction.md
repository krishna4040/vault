There are two ways for seduction to happen:

1. A man creates the perception that he is far less [[Sexual Investment|Invested]] in her than he actually is (neediness disguised as non neediness)
2. A man actually is less invested in her (genuine non neediness)

The first method (a man giving false impressions) occurs through what I call performance.
Performance-based dating advice technically “works.” It’s just not fulfilling
A man who becomes successful through a series of performance-based behaviors may have sex, but he is setting himself up for terrible and unfulfilling relationships with other needy women.
