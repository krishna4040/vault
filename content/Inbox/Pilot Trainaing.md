## Licensing in India

### How?

1. **Register with DGCA** as a student pilot.
2. **Self-study** (use DGCA books/online resources).
3. **Apply for exams** via DGCA’s **PARIKH** portal.
4. **Clear exams** → Join flying school for practical training.

> [!tip]
> clear the CPL exams directly

### Validity?

- You must complete **flight training and skill test within 5 years**, or rewrite exams.
- **RTR (Radio License):** **Lifetime validity** (no renewal needed).
- **Medical Certificates:**
  - **Class 2 (PPL):** 2 years (if <40 years) / 1 year (if >40).
  - **Class 1 (CPL):** 1 year (renewable).

### Passing Criteria & Attempts

- **Passing Marks:** **70%** in each subject.
- **Attempts:** Unlimited, but must wait **30 days** between retakes.

### Cost & Time for Exams

- **Exam Fees:** ~₹1,500-2,000 per subject.
- **RTR Exam:** ~₹3,000.
- **Study Time:**
  - **PPL:** 3-6 months (self-study).
  - **CPL:** 6-12 months (due to extra subjects).

**Currency and Validity**

| Concept      | Meaning                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Validity** | How long the license is legally valid      |
| **Currency** | Whether you can **actually fly** (recency) |
So even if your CPL is **valid for 5 years** (DGCA), you can’t fly unless you’re **"current"** — i.e., you've flown recently and passed check rides.

**License Renewal**

# Case studies

### **Collapse of Jet Airways & Kingfisher Airlines: Reasons, Impact on Jobs, and Lessons for Aspiring Pilots**

The failure of **Jet Airways (2019)** and **Kingfisher Airlines (2012)** was a major shock to India’s aviation sector, leading to **mass layoffs, reduced salaries, and long-term distrust in airline stability**. Here’s a breakdown of what happened, its impact, and key lessons for aspiring pilots.

### **Kingfisher Airlines (2005–2012)**

- **Reasons for Collapse:**
  - **Massive Debt (~₹7,000 Cr+)** – Aggressive expansion without profits.
  - **High Operating Costs** – Luxury services (free alcohol, premium seats) drained cash.
  - **Fuel Dues & Employee Salaries Unpaid** – Banks froze accounts.
  - **Government Denied Bailout** – Vijay Mallya fled India (financial fraud case).

### **Jet Airways (1993–2019)**

- **Reasons for Collapse:**
  - **Debt (~₹8,000 Cr+)** – Failed to compete with low-cost carriers (IndiGo, SpiceJet).
  - **Poor Management** – Bought expensive planes (Boeing 777s) but couldn’t fill seats.
  - **Stopped Paying Pilots & Vendors** – Pilots went on strike (2019).
  - **No Buyer for Revival** – Banks took control, but no investor stepped in.

## **2. Impact on Aviation Jobs**

### **Immediate Effects (2012 & 2019)**

✔ **Thousands of Pilots & Crew Unemployed** – Jet alone had **16,000 employees**.\
✔ **Salaries Dropped** – Airlines hired ex-Jet/Kingfisher pilots at lower wages.\
✔ **Hiring Freeze** – IndiGo/SpiceJet slowed recruitment due to market uncertainty.

### **Long-Term Effects (Even Today)**

✔ **Airlines Now Prefer Cost-Cutting** – Fewer perks, more contract-based hiring.\
✔ **Pilot Unions Stronger** – Now demand salary guarantees.\
✔ **DGCA Tightened Financial Checks** – Airlines must prove salary payment capability.

## **3. Key Lessons for Aspiring Pilots**

### **Before Entering the Industry, Monitor:**

✅ **Airline Financial Health**

- Check if the airline is **profitable** (e.g., IndiGo, Air India under Tata).
- Avoid airlines with **high debt** (like Go First, which collapsed in 2023).

✅ **Job Security & Salary Trends**

- Prefer airlines with **on-time salary payments** (e.g., IndiGo, Vistara).
- Avoid startups with **no clear funding** (e.g., Akasa is new but backed by investors).

✅ **Industry Risks**

- **Fuel Prices & Rupee-Dollar Rate** – Affects airline profits.
- **Government Policies** – Open Skies, FDI rules impact competition.

✅ **Backup Plan**

- Get **instructor ratings (CFI)** or **corporate pilot licenses** for alternative jobs.
- Consider **Middle East/SE Asia jobs** if Indian market slows down.

## **4. What to Continuously Monitor?**

📌 **Airlines’ Quarterly Profit/Loss Reports** (e.g., IndiGo’s investor relations page).\
📌 **DGCA & Govt. Policies** (e.g., UDAN scheme, new airport approvals).\
📌 **Pilot Demand vs. Supply** – Too many CPL holders = lower salaries.\
📌 **Global Aviation Trends** – Boeing/Airbus orders indicate future job openings.

### **Final Verdict**

- **Aviation is a high-risk, high-reward career** – Jet/Kingfisher’s collapse shows the dangers of **poor management & debt**.
- **Choose airlines wisely** – Prioritize **financial stability** over glamour.
- **Always have an exit plan** – Instructor ratings, foreign licenses help in downturns.
