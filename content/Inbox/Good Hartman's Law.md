# What I found

`Goodhart's` law: "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure."

# Why I liked it

It explains how a metric can stop representing the outcome we actually care about when people start optimizing for the metric itself.

# My initial thought

This might explain why optimizing a measurable proxy can sometimes produce worse real-world outcomes.
